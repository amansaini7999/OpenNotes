OpenNotes gives your ideas a place to grow. Write down a thought, sketch a plan,
or bring images and PDFs into your notebook. Use it in your browser or install
it as a web app, then keep working offline.

## Features

- **Handwritten notes** with pressure-sensitive ink, highlighting, erasing,
  and undo/redo.
- **Organized notebooks** with nested folders, colorful covers, and paper styles.
- **Images and PDF books** up to 1,000 PDF pages, plus pasted screenshots within notes.
- **A focused workspace** with read mode and swipe or vertical page navigation.
- **Offline use** after the initial setup, without a backend or subscription.
- **Editable backups and PDF export** so you can keep and share your work.

## Your notes

Notebooks are stored locally in your browser, not uploaded to an OpenNotes
server. There is no automatic cloud sync. Export backups regularly: clearing
browser data or storage eviction can remove local notes.

See the [installation guide](./docs/INSTALLATION.md) to get started and the
[user guide](./docs/USER_GUIDE.md) for writing, navigation, and backups.
